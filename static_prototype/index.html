Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know

Click the "Register Now" button on any tournament card. Fill in your team details including Team Name, Captain Name, Free Fire UID, and Mobile Number. Select your tournament type (Solo, Duo, or Squad) and complete the entry fee payment. Upload a screenshot of payment and submit your registration. You'll receive confirmation within 24 hours.
Entry fees can be paid via UPI, Google Pay, PhonePe, or Paytm. After selecting your tournament, you'll see the payment details. Make the payment to the provided UPI ID and upload a screenshot as proof. Our team will verify payment and approve your registration within a few hours.
Winners are determined through a points-based system combining Kill Points and Placement Points. Teams progress through Qualifiers → Quarter Finals → Semi Finals → Grand Final. The team with the highest total points in the Grand Final is crowned the champion. Detailed scoring rules are shared before every tournament.
Prizes are distributed within 24-48 hours after the tournament results are published and verified. Winners will receive prize money directly to their UPI-linked bank account or wallet. A breakdown of prizes is shared on the results page for full transparency.
Team changes are allowed up to 2 hours before the tournament starts. Contact our support team via WhatsApp or Discord with your registration ID and the details of the changes you'd like to make. Note that the captain cannot be changed after registration.
